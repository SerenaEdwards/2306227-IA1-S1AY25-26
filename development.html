"Twelve Pillars" by Jim Rohn and Chris Widener

Chapter 4 – Achieve Your Goals

In this chapter, Michael learns from Charlie that success begins with setting clear and specific goals. Charlie explains that many people go through life without really knowing what they want, but those who succeed have a vision and a plan to reach it. He encourages writing goals down and breaking them into small steps that can be worked on every day.This made me realize how important it is to know exactly what I’m working toward. I can apply this by writing down my own goals and being intentional about them. Whether it’s finishing school, saving money, or starting a business, I need to have a plan instead of just hoping things work out. Setting goals gives me something to aim for and helps me stay focused when life gets distracting.

Chapter 5 – The Proper Use of Time

Charlie talks about how time is one of the most valuable things we have. Everyone gets the same 24 hours in a day, but how we use it makes the difference between success and failure. He teaches Michael that it’s easy to waste time on unimportant things, so we must learn to plan our days and make time for what truly matters. I really connected with this because I often find myself wasting time on my phone or putting things off. This chapter reminded me that time lost can never be regained. I want to start managing my time better by setting priorities and using my days wisely. If I can control how I spend my time, I can achieve more and feel less stressed.

Chapter 6 – Surround Yourself with the Best People

In this chapter, Charlie explains that the people we spend time with have a big influence on who we become. If we’re always around negative people, we’ll start thinking and acting like them. But if we surround ourselves with positive, motivated people, we’ll grow and improve. This really hit home for me. I’ve noticed that when I’m around people who are focused and driven, I feel more motivated too. It reminded me to be careful about who I let into my circle. I want to spend more time with people who encourage me and believe in doing their best. At the same time, I want to be someone who brings out the best in others.

Chapter 7 – Be a Lifelong Learner

Charlie tells Michael that learning shouldn’t stop after school. Successful people never stop growing — they read, study, and learn from others every day. He encourages building a personal library and always finding ways to improve. This chapter reminded me that growth doesn’t end when we finish our studies. There’s always something new to learn, whether it’s through books, experiences, or other people. I want to make learning a part of my daily life, not just for school but for my own personal development. The more I learn, the more I can improve myself and be ready for new opportunities.

Overall Reflection

These chapters really made me think about the kind of person I want to become. They show that success isn’t just about luck — it’s about setting goals, using time wisely, choosing the right people, and always learning. I can see how each lesson connects to real life, and I want to start applying them in small ways every day. If I can do that, I believe I’ll keep growing into a better, more focused, and more positive version of myself.
